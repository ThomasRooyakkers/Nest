## 1. Screen recording (Thomas, on the iPhone, latest iOS)

Use the screen recording in Control Center, with the microphone off. It
should take about 3–4 minutes. Do it in this order:

1. Start on the home screen and **launch the app** (Apple wants the
   recording to start at launch).
2. Sign-in screen: show that the server is `nest.g3cko.be`.
3. **Registration:** tap *Sign in with Apple* with an Apple ID that has no
   Nest account yet (a new account, no invite needed). Create a household
   when the app asks.
4. Briefly show each tab (Home, Shopping, Meals, Tasks, Watch): add a
   shopping item, tick it off, add a task.
5. **Account deletion:** Settings → *Delete account* → *Delete for good*.
   Use the new Apple account here, **never the demo account**.
6. **Login:** sign in with the demo account (`verification@g3cko.be` and
   its password). Show the sample data: shopping tiles, the meal plan, a
   recipe (servings stepper, cooking mode), tasks, the watchlist.
7. **User content is private:** Settings → household → *Invite someone* (the
   code) and *Leave household*. This shows content is shared only with
   invited family members.

Trim the video in Photos and keep it under about 500 MB. Attach it to the
reply below, and also as the *Attachment* under App Review Information.

```
Hi,

Thanks for looking at Hoomly, and for the clear list. I'm the developer and I've answered each point below; the same information is now in the App Review Notes.

1. Screen recording
Attached is a recording from my own iPhone, on the latest iOS and the submitted build (1.0.0, build 1). It starts at launch and shows creating an account with Sign in with Apple, the five tabs (Home, Shopping, Meals, Tasks, Watch), deleting that account in Settings > Delete account, and signing in with the demo account. My phone is set to Dutch, so you'll see the Dutch version; the app follows the device language. There is no paid content: no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

2. Purpose and audience
Hoomly ("Nest" on the home screen) is a household organiser for families and anyone who shares a home. I built it for my own family, because our household ran on group chats, paper lists and three different apps, and things kept slipping. It brings together a shared shopping list that updates live and works offline, a weekly dinner plan, a recipe book that rescales servings and has a cooking mode, shared chores with due dates and repeats, and a watchlist for films and series. It's free, with no ads or tracking.

3. Getting started
No setup needed: the app uses our server, nest.g3cko.be, by default. Please sign in with the demo account in the Sign-In Information. Its household has sample content on every screen: tap a shopping tile to mark it bought, plan dinner on an empty day, open a recipe and try the servings buttons or cooking mode, tick off a task, or let the Watch tab pick something for tonight. Settings is the gear icon on Home.
- New accounts: Sign in with Apple or Google creates one straight away. Email sign-up needs an invite code from a household (Settings > Invite someone).
- Account deletion: Settings > Delete account. This permanently removes the account and its data.
- "Change server" on the sign-in screen isn't needed; it's for people who run their own Nest server.

User content: what people add (shopping items, recipes, tasks, watchlist titles) is only visible to members of their own household, and you can only join with an invite code from a member. There is no public feed, no searchable profiles, no messaging and no way to contact anyone outside your household. Anyone can leave a household at any time (Settings > Leave household).

4. External services
- Our own Nest server (Serverpod and PostgreSQL), run by me, over HTTPS through Cloudflare
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In
- Resend, for email codes when signing up with email or resetting a password
- The TMDB API, for film and series search, posters and ratings (called by our server only)
No payment processors, AI services, advertising or analytics.

5. Regional differences
None. The app works the same everywhere, in English and Dutch.

6. Regulated industries and third-party material
Not a regulated industry. The only third-party material is film and series data and posters from TMDB, used under TMDB's API terms, with their required credit in the app (Watch tab and About screen) and in the description.

If anything is unclear or you'd like to see more, just let me know.

Kind regards,
Thomas Rooyakkers
```

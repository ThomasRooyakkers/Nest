## 1. Screen recording (Thomas, on the iPhone, latest iOS)

Use the screen recording in Control Center, with the microphone off. It
should take about 3–4 minutes. Do it in this order:

1. Start on the home screen and **launch the app** (Apple wants the
   recording to start at launch).
2. Sign-in screen: show that the server is `nest.g3cko.be`.
3. **Registration:** tap *Sign in with Apple* with an Apple ID that has no
   Nest account yet (a new account, no invite needed). Create a household
   when the app asks.
4. Briefly show each tab (Home, Shopping, Meals, Tasks, Watch): add a
   shopping item, tick it off, add a task.
5. **Account deletion:** Settings → *Delete account* → *Delete for good*.
   Use the new Apple account here, **never the demo account**.
6. **Login:** sign in with the demo account (`verification@g3cko.be` and
   its password). Show the sample data: shopping tiles, the meal plan, a
   recipe (servings stepper, cooking mode), tasks, the watchlist.
7. **User content is private:** Settings → household → *Invite someone* (the
   code) and *Leave household*. This shows content is shared only with
   invited family members.

Trim the video in Photos and keep it under about 500 MB. Attach it to the
reply below, and also as the *Attachment* under App Review Information.

```
Hi,

Thanks for taking the time to look at Hoomly, and for the clear list of what you need. I'm the developer, and I'm happy to walk you through it. I've answered each point below. The same information is now also in the App Review Notes.

1. Screen recording

Attached is a screen recording made on my own iPhone, running the latest iOS and the same build I submitted (1.0.0, build 1). It starts with launching the app and goes through the normal flow:
- creating a new account with Sign in with Apple and setting up a household
- the five main tabs: Home, Shopping, Meals, Tasks and Watch
- deleting that account again from Settings > Delete account
- signing in with the demo account, which has sample data in every section

I've also attached a few screenshots from my phone. My phone is set to Dutch, so you'll see the Dutch version of the app; it follows the device language.

There is no paid content in the app: no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

2. What the app is for, and who it's for

Hoomly (it shows as "Nest" on the home screen) is a household organiser for families and anyone who shares a home. I built it for my own family because our household ran on a mix of group chats, paper lists and three different apps, and things kept falling through the cracks.

It brings that together in one place:
- a shared shopping list that updates live on everyone's phone and also works offline in the shop
- a weekly dinner plan
- a recipe book where you can change the number of servings and the amounts follow, with a cooking mode that keeps the screen on
- shared chores with a due date, someone assigned to them and repeats (daily, weekly, monthly)
- a watchlist of films and series, so the "what shall we watch tonight" discussion gets a bit shorter

It's free. There are no ads and no tracking, and the family's data stays on our own server, not with advertisers.

3. How to get started

No setup is needed. The app connects to our server, nest.g3cko.be, by default.

Please sign in with the demo account in the Sign-In Information field (email and password). It belongs to a household that already has sample content, so every screen has something to show:
- Home: tonight's dinner, what's on the shopping list, today's tasks and the next title on the watchlist
- Shopping: tap a tile to mark it as bought; type in "What do you need?" to add something
- Meals: browse the week and tap "+ Plan dinner" on an empty day
- Recipes: open one from Home or Meals; try the servings buttons, cooking mode and "Add to list"
- Tasks: filter with the chips at the top, tick a task off, or add one with the "+" button
- Watch: search for a film or series, switch between the status tabs, or tap "Pick something for tonight"
- Settings (gear icon on Home): language, household, invite code, Leave household and Delete account

Creating a new account: Sign in with Apple or Google creates one straight away. Signing up with email and a password needs an invite code from a household (Settings > Invite someone), because households are private.

Deleting an account: Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes the account and the user's data. If the user was the only member of a household, that household is deleted too.

You'll also see "Change server" on the sign-in screen. You don't need it. It's only for people who run their own Nest server instead of using ours.

About content created by users: everything people add (shopping items, recipes, tasks, watchlist titles) is only visible to the members of their own household. You can only join a household with an invite code from someone who is already in it. There is no public feed, no searchable profiles, no chat or messaging, and no way to see or contact anyone outside your own household. Anyone can leave a household at any time (Settings > Leave household), and deleting your account removes your data.

4. External services the app uses

- Our own Nest server: the backend, built with Serverpod and PostgreSQL. I run it myself; it is reached over HTTPS through Cloudflare.
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: optional ways to sign in.
- Resend: sends the email codes for signing up with email and resetting a password.
- TMDB (The Movie Database) API: the film and series search, posters and ratings. Only our server talks to TMDB; the app never does.

The app does not use payment processors, AI services, advertising or analytics.

5. Regional differences

There are none. The app works the same in every region. It's available in English and Dutch and follows the device language.

6. Regulated industries and third-party material

Hoomly isn't in a regulated industry. The only third-party material is the film and series information and posters from TMDB. We use the TMDB API under TMDB's terms of use and show their required credit in the app (on the Watch tab and the About screen) and in the App Store description: "This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB."

If anything is unclear or you'd like to see something else, just let me know and I'll reply quickly.

Kind regards,
Thomas Rooyakkers
```
